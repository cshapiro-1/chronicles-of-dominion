# Chronicles of Dominion: Permanent Art & Asset Policy

## Goal Visual Target
Dense, lived-in Mythic Grimdark Bronze Age Mesopotamian city. Monumental stepped ziggurat as the clear centerpiece, wide river, extensive visible farmland, packed mudbrick housing, colorful market activity, armies on roads, elevated three-quarter camera view. Reference the high-quality Akkadian city image provided by the Director.

## Core Rules
1. You are allowed to create simple low-poly assets yourself.
2. You are never allowed to use the old procedural noise / stacked primitive style that looked like a greybox prototype.
3. Every single asset must go through the approval process below. No exceptions.
4. Build everything in small, reviewable chunks. Never create more than one major asset (or a very small related set) before stopping for approval.
5. Prefer clean geometric forms, readable silhouettes, and earthy materials:
   - Primary: Warm mudbrick (#D8BA8C / #B5946A), sun-baked clay, dark cedar wood.
   - Accents: Aged hammered bronze (#C8963E), royal lapis lazuli glazed brick (#1E4287), muted woven linen/canvas canopies (terracotta, indigo, saffron).

## Approval Workflow (Mandatory)
For each asset:
1. **Propose**: Name, intended purpose, geometric concept, material palette.
2. **Create**: Place the single asset in 
es://assets/generated/pending_approval/.
3. **Present**: Show the asset cleanly in isolation with material details and dimensions.
4. **Stop & Wait**: End your turn with:
   Waiting for Director approval.
   Do NOT proceed to the next asset or add it to the main game scene until the Director explicitly replies with approval.

## Priority Order for Building the City
1. **Ziggurat Core** (stepped base + summit shrine)
2. **City Walls & Gates** (straight segment, corner tower, gatehouse)
3. **Housing** (mudbrick houses 2-3 variants, courtyard house)
4. **Farmland** (crop field tile)
5. **Civic / Market** (market stalls with colored canopies, granary)
6. **Units** (spearman, slinger, cart, raider)
7. **Environment** (palm tree, river bank, roads)
